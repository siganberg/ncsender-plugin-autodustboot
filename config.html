Control

How your dust boot is connected, and when it moves on its own.

Wired: a relay on your controller's Flood or Mist output. Wireless: the AutoDustBoot controller, which you can also move from here.

Wired Wireless
Wire your relay's signal to one of the CNC controller outputs — Flood (M8/M9) or Mist (M7/M9) work well. The G-code sequences below toggle that output to raise and lower the dust boot.

Retract runs before homing (and, if enabled below, before rapid moves). Expand runs after the sequence completes. Anything valid in your controller's G-code is fair game — use dwell commands (G4) to let the boot finish its stroke before motion continues.

Options

Retract on Home

Lifts the boot before the machine homes, so it can't get bumped.

Retract on Rapid Moves

Lifts the boot for "move to" and other rapid moves outside a job. Cuts are never interrupted.

Safe zones

Where something sticks up, like clamps or extrusions on the sides. In a zone the boot rides at its safe height, then drops back when the spindle leaves. The job keeps running.

Safe zones need the wireless AutoDustBoot: a wired boot only has up and down, with no height in between. Switch to Wireless on the Control tab.

What happens during a pass

  1. You mark the zones

    Draw a box on the bed map wherever something sticks up, or jog the spindle to its corners.

  2. You set one safe height

    Move the boot until it clears the tallest thing in your zones. Keep it as low as you can for dust.

  3. The boot lifts before the zone

    ncSender reads the program ahead and times the lift from your machine's speeds, so the boot is up before the spindle gets there.

  4. It drops back after

    When the spindle leaves, the boot expands again. The spindle never stops. If the boot is ever late, the job holds until it's up.

Beta: test it before you cut.
  1. Take the lower dust shoe off for your first runs, so nothing can catch if the boot is late.
  2. Do an air cut: set Z zero well above the stock and run the program. Watch the boot lift before each zone and drop after it.
  3. Keep a hand near Feed Hold. When every pass looks right, put the shoe back on.
