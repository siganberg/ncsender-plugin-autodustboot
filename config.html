Connections

Choose how your dust boot is connected.

Pick Wired if your dust boot is controlled by a relay wired into your CNC. Pick Wireless if you have the AutoDustBoot wireless controller — you'll also be able to move it up and down right from this panel.

Wired Wireless
Wire your relay's signal to one of the CNC controller outputs — Flood (M8/M9) or Mist (M7/M9) work well. The G-code sequences below toggle that output to raise and lower the dust boot.

Retract runs before homing (and, if enabled below, before rapid moves). Expand runs after the sequence completes. Anything valid in your controller's G-code is fair game — use dwell commands (G4) to let the boot finish its stroke before motion continues.

Options

Handy behaviors you can turn on or off.

These settings decide when your dust boot lifts out of the way automatically. Click Save when you're done.

Retract on Home

Lifts the dust boot out of the way before your machine homes, so it doesn't get bumped or damaged. Leave this on unless you have a specific reason to handle it yourself.

Retract on Rapid Moves

Lifts the dust boot when you use the "move to" or similar rapid commands to reposition the spindle. Only applies outside of a job — your cuts are never interrupted.

Max Travel

mm

Physical extension distance of your AutoDustBoot, set by the device firmware. Adjust here if you're running a modified firmware with a different limit — this only affects the position readouts and slider bounds in this dialog. Default is 92 mm.

Show in Terminal

Shows every command this plugin sends in the terminal, so you can see what's happening. Useful when setting things up, distracting for everyday use — leave off unless you're troubleshooting.

Unpair Device

Are you sure you want to unpair this AutoDustBoot? You will need to re-pair it before it can receive wireless commands again.
